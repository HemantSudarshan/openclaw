## 2024-07-12 - Weak Randomness in ID Generation\n**Vulnerability:** Weak random number generation (`Math.random()`) used for attachment IDs in UI.\n**Learning:** The guardrail `check-temp-path-guardrails.ts` warns against `Math.random` combined with `Date.now` for IDs. In the frontend, `ui/src/ui/uuid.ts` provides a secure `generateUUID` function.\n**Prevention:** Avoid `Math.random()` for identifier generation and use secure wrappers instead.
